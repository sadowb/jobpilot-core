# Greenhouse
- An embedded form may sit in an iframe on the company site: open `job-boards.greenhouse.io/<tenant>/jobs/<id>` instead.
- Resume is the file input labelled Resume/CV; "Enter manually" text areas are not needed.
- Education/graduation dropdowns often lack exact months: map to the closest truthful option only from a confirmed answer, else ask.
- An email verification step can come before the final one.
